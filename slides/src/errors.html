Two kinds of error

Factuality

The output conflicts with world knowledge.

“BRCA1 is on chromosome 13.” It is on 17; BRCA2 is on 13.

Faithfulness

The output does not follow the given source or instruction.

A summary that adds a number the abstract does not contain.

Huang et al. 2025, ACM TOIS · Ji et al. 2023, ACM Computing Surveys

Both matter in research. Faithfulness errors are especially dangerous in literature work and data extraction, because the source is right there and we assume the model used it.
